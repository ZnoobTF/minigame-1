# Minigame 1
## Devlog
A metaphor for the relationship between Components, GameObjects, and Scenes is a zoo. 

The zoo itself is the Scene, with the animals inside that zoo being the GameObjects, and the Components are the traits of the animal, like the makeup of their body, their behaviour, and more.
